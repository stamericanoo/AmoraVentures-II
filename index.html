Process Overview

1. Sulfur Burning Furnace

Sulfur is combusted in dry air producing SO₂. Highly exothermic reaction releases -296 kJ/mol. Operating temperature: 900–1100°C.

2. Gas Cleaning & Drying

Removal of particulates and moisture using electrostatic precipitators and drying towers to protect catalyst activity.

3. Catalytic Converter

SO₂ oxidized to SO₃ using V₂O₅ catalyst at 420–450°C. Equilibrium carefully controlled for maximum yield.

4. Absorption Tower

SO₃ absorbed in concentrated H₂SO₄ to form oleum. Direct water absorption avoided to prevent acid mist formation.

5. Dilution Section

Oleum diluted under temperature control to produce commercial-grade sulfuric acid.
